# Real-person test kit ("A real person tries it" is a required rule)

**Who:** anyone who runs a small business or side hustle that takes card / e-wallet payments (a café, kuih seller,
online shop, tuition centre, a family business, a campus club that sells merch).

## Before (2 minutes, voice note or video)
Ask, and record the answer with permission:
1. "How do you check that your sales record matches what Stripe / TnG / the bank actually paid you?"
2. "How long does that take you each month? What goes wrong?"

## Try it (5 minutes)
Send them the public dashboard link. Ask them to:
1. Read the top summary and say what they think happened this month.
2. Open one item under **Needs your decision**, read the evidence, and approve or reject it.
3. Look at **Ledger diff**.
4. Open **Try another month** and press *Generate a new random month* to see the crew handle data it has never seen.

**Fake data only** (track rule): never ask them to upload real sales or bank exports. Their real experience goes in the conversation, not the files.

## After (1 minute)
- "Would you trust this to fix the small things on its own? Which caps would you set?"
- "What's missing before you'd use it every month?"

## Consent line to read out / send
> "Can I use a short clip or a quote of your reaction in my hackathon submission on Devpost? I won't share
> your business's real numbers." — save their "yes" (screenshot of the chat is fine).

Put their quote in `docs/SUBMISSION.md` and the clip in the demo video. If they ask for a change, that's a
great extra item to ship and mention.
